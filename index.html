VitalLabs viewer

A static, local-first interface for reviewing multisensor sleep-monitoring recordings.

Signals

Optical or SpO2, BPM, microphone, GSR/EDA, timestamps, notes, and event labels.

Privacy

Files are parsed inside the browser. No account, server, or data upload is used.

Use

For research visualization only. Sensor output requires validation before medical interpretation.
